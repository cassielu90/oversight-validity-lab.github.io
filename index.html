GAP 01

Tests are recognisable

At 0.89 AUROC, clean sandbox behaviour cannot be assumed to hold in deployment.

GAP 02

Coverage ≠ sensitivity

High monitor routing coverage does not reveal how often serious misconduct is missed.

GAP 03

Reviewers are part of the system

Flag-only reviewers miss unflagged failures and are vulnerable to sycophantic framing.

03 · Impact

Theory of Change

01 · Frontier Labs

Labs & embedded evaluators

  • Replace unqualified system-card assurances with restricted, measured claims.
  • Set review cadence from measured miss rates via external eval teams & AI Evaluator Forum.
02 · Deployers

Organisations deploying agents

  • Restrict permission setups that reproduce incident mechanisms.
  • Place human review where it demonstrably catches violations via our decision pilot.
03 · Governance

Regulators & standards bodies

  • Ask for deployment-side oversight evidence in incident & conformity reporting.
  • Adopt a draft reporting template backed by UK AISI’s Inspect framework.
